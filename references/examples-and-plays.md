# Examples & Situation-Specific Plays

## Salary / Job Offer
- **BATNA**: Other offers, staying in current role, freelancing runway.
- **Matrix**: Usually Collaborate or Compromise (relationship + ongoing employment matters).
- **Interests**: Compensation, growth, flexibility, title, impact — not just base salary.
- **Play**: Expand the package (bonus, equity, remote days, learning budget) to enlarge ZOPA.

## Vendor / Supplier Contract
- **BATNA**: Alternative suppliers or in-house option.
- **Matrix**: Compete if pure price, Collaborate if strategic long-term supply.
- **Play**: Trade volume commitments or longer term for better unit price / SLAs.

## Partnership / Joint Venture
- **Matrix**: Strongly Collaborate.
- **Play**: Map both parties’ strategic interests first. Create joint upside mechanisms.

## Conflict / Dispute Resolution
- **Matrix**: Often Collaborate or Accommodate depending on future relationship value.
- **Play**: Separate people from problem early. Use objective criteria (market data, past precedent).

## One-time High-Stakes Deal (e.g. asset sale)
- **Matrix**: Compete (outcome-focused).
- **Play**: Strong BATNA is everything. Anchor high but leave room inside ZOPA. Prepare walk-away language.

## Internal Resource Negotiation (budget, headcount)
- **Interests**: Organizational goals, risk, timeline, political capital.
- **Play**: Frame requests in terms of company priorities (Harvard method). Offer options that reduce the other side’s risk.

## Sample Prep Questions (Information Exchange)
- “What does success look like for you in this agreement?”
- “What constraints are you working under?”
- “Why is that particular term important to you?”
- “If we can’t agree on X, what alternatives would still work for you?”
- “What would make this a clear win for your side?”
- “Are there any non-monetary things that matter a lot here?”

## Expanding the ZOPA
- Add variables (payment terms, scope, exclusivity, support, future options).
- Trade low-cost/high-value items.
- Introduce contingent agreements (“if X happens, then Y”).
- Bring in objective standards both sides accept.
