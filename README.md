# Negotiation Frameworks — Grok Skill

A reusable Grok skill that teaches proven negotiation frameworks and techniques for stronger deals:

1. **4 Phases** (Lewicki) — Preparation → Information Exchange → Bargaining → Commitment
2. **IFORESAW / I FORESAW IT** (Seth Freeman) — Comprehensive preparation checklist
3. **BATNA** (Fisher & Ury) — Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement
4. **ZOPA** (Fisher & Ury) — Zone of Possible Agreement
5. **Harvard Principled Negotiation** (Fisher, Ury & Patton) — Interests over positions
6. **Negotiation Matrix** (Lewicki & Hiam) — Choose strategy by outcome vs relationship
7. **Chris Voss Tactical Empathy** (FBI) — Mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions, accusation audit, Ackerman bargaining

## What’s inside

## How to use with Grok

Place the skill folder in your Grok skills directory. It will load automatically on questions about negotiations, BATNA, ZOPA, deal preparation, salary talks, FBI-style tactics, tactical empathy, etc.

Ask things like:
- “Help me prepare for a salary negotiation”
- “What’s my BATNA here and how do I improve it?”
- “Walk me through the 4 phases + Voss moves for this vendor deal”
- “Give me the one-page negotiation prep template”
- “How would Chris Voss handle this conversation?”

**Core ideas**:  
Great negotiators are the most prepared, not the most aggressive.  
Curiosity beats persuasion.  
Preparation creates leverage.  
Best deals happen when both sides feel heard — and the agreement can actually be implemented.
